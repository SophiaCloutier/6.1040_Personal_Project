# Entry 1: Design Notes
I like where this is going and it seems to be fleshing out like how I had it in my mind which I am pleased with. However, the more I think about it, I am worried that the OBO feature is going to be very complicated to implement. In terms of design choices, I stayed pretty true to my original problem framing (the only part of that that I changed was to find more concrete evidence of the bad situations I was describing) and most of the innovation for this step came from the concept and reaction specifications.
