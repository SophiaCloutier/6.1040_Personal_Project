# User Journey 1: Buying an Item
Percy is an MIT student looking for a reading chair for his dorm room. Normally, finding one through senior sales would mean checking dormspam, opening multiple PowerPoints, and hoping an item is still available by the time they pay.
Percy opens Marketplace, searches for furniture, and finds Sophia’s $24 reading chair. He click the listing to open the item page, where he can see the photos, condition, description, pickup location, and seller information. After deciding to buy it, Percy clicks “Claim for $24.”
The chair is temporarily reserved while Percy pays. Claimed! shows Sophia’s Venmo or Zelle information, and Percy sends the payment. They return to the app and click “I’ve paid” to mark the payment as sent.
Percy then opens My activity and stays on the Buying tab. The chair shows that the item is reserved, payment has been sent, and the sale is waiting for Sophia’s confirmation. Once Sophia confirms the payment, the status moves to Arrange pickup. Percy clicks “Message seller” to coordinate pickup at Maseeh Hall, then picks up the chair.

# User Journey 2: Selling an Item
Sophia is graduating and wants to sell a reading chair before moving out. Traditionally, she would need to make a PowerPoint, send dormspam, update the slide when the chair is claimed, monitor payments, and coordinate pickup separately.
Sophia clicks Sell an item in the navigation bar. She clicks Add photos, uploads a picture of the chair, enters “Reading chair,” selects Furniture, enters $24, adds a description, chooses Maseeh Hall as the pickup location, and leaves the sale type as Fixed price. She then clicks “Publish listing.”
Sophia opens My activity and clicks the Selling tab. The chair appears in Your listings with an Active status. When Percy claims it, the status changes to Reserved.
After Percy reports that payment was sent, Sophia clicks “Manage” on the reading chair listing. She checks Venmo or Zelle, then clicks “Confirm payment received.” The sale is marked complete, and Sophia uses the message area to arrange pickup with Percy.
After Percy picks up the chair, the listing appears as Sold in My activity → Selling.
