# Stakeholders
**Buyers:** MIT students looking to find and purchase items from senior sales.
**Sellers:** Students trying to sell or give away unwanted items with as little hassle as possible.
**Other interested buyers:** Students waiting to see whether an item becomes available after someone else claims it.
**Dorm staff / residential community:** People who may support or be affected by item pickup and drop-off in dorms.
**External service providers:** Services like dormspam, Venmo, and Zelle that currently support advertising, communication, and payment.
