# Concepts
## Concept 1: Listing

**concept** Listing [Seller]\
**purpose** make items available for others to discover while allowing their seller to keep the information about them current\
**principle** a seller lists an item with information describing it, and the seller may later update the information or remove the item

**state**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;a set of Listings with\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; seller Seller\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a title String\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a description String\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a price Number\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a category String\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;an optional size String\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;an image String\

**actions**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;create (seller: Seller, title: String, description: String, price: Number, category: String, size: String, image: String) : (listing: Listing)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;create and return a listing with the supplied information
            
&nbsp;&nbsp;update (listing: Listing, title: String, description: String, price: Number, category: String, size: String, image: String)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;listing exists\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;replace the listing's information with the supplied information

&nbsp;&nbsp;remove (listing: Listing)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;listing exists\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;remove the listing
            
## Concept 2: Claiming

**concept** Claiming [Claimer, Resource]\
**purpose** give one person temporary exclusive access to a resource; prevents multiple people from simultaneously acting as though they have secured the same resource\
**principle** a person claims an available resource and gains exclusive access to it; the claim can later be completed or released, after which the resource is respectively unavailable or available again

**state**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;a set of Resources with\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a status Status\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;an optional claimer Claimer

**types**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Status is available or claimed or completed

**actions**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;makeAvailable (resource: Resource)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;resource is not already in Resources\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;add resource with status available

&nbsp;&nbsp;claim (claimer: Claimer, resource: Resource)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;resource has status available\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;set resource's status to claimed\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;set its claimer to claimer

&nbsp;&nbsp;release (claimer: Claimer, resource: Resource)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;resource has status claimed\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;claimer is the resource's claimer\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;set resource's status to available\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;remove its claimer

&nbsp;&nbsp;complete (claimer: Claimer, resource: Resource)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;resource has status claimed\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;claimer is the resource's claimer\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;set resource's status to completed

## Concept 3: Offering

**concept** Offering [Offerer, Resource]\
**purpose** let people compete for a resource without requiring the owner to privately coordinate competing proposals\
**principle** people make offers for a resource; an offer may be changed or withdrawn while offers are being considered; one offer may ultimately be accepted

**state**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;a set of Offers with\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;an offerer Offerer\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a resource Resource\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;an amount Number\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a status OfferStatus

**types**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;OfferStatus is pending or accepted or withdrawn or rejected

**actions**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;make (offerer: Offerer, resource: Resource, amount: Number)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;offerer has no pending offer for resource\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;create a pending offer for offerer, resource, and amount

&nbsp;&nbsp;revise (offerer: Offerer, resource: Resource, amount: Number)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a pending offer exists for offerer and resource\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;replace the amount of that offer

&nbsp;&nbsp;withdraw (offerer: Offerer, resource: Resource)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a pending offer exists for offerer and resource\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;set the offer's status to withdrawn

&nbsp;&nbsp;accept (offerer: Offerer, resource: Resource)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a pending offer exists for offerer and resource\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;no offer for resource has been accepted\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;set that offer's status to accepted\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;set all other pending offers for resource to rejected


## Concept 4: TransactionConfirming

**concept** TransactionConfirming [Payer, Payee, Subject]\
**purpose** establish shared confirmation that an external transaction occurred; prevents either party from mistaking an unconfirmed transaction for a completed one\
**principle** a payer reports that they have paid for a subject; the payee then confirms receiving the payment, completing the confirmation

**state**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;a set of Transactions with\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a payer Payer\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a payee Payee\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a subject Subject\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a status TransactionStatus

**types**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;TransactionStatus is awaitingPayment or awaitingConfirmation or confirmed

**actions**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;start (payer: Payer, payee: Payee, subject: Subject)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;no transaction exists for subject\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;create a transaction with status awaitingPayment

&nbsp;&nbsp;reportPaid (payer: Payer, subject: Subject)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;transaction for payer and subject has status awaitingPayment\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;set its status to awaitingConfirmation

&nbsp;&nbsp;confirmReceived (payee: Payee, subject: Subject)\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;transaction for subject has status awaitingConfirmation\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;payee is its payee\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;set its status to confirmed

# Reactions

**reaction** makeListingAvailable\
**when**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Listing.create(...): (listing)\
**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Claiming.makeAvailable(resource: listing)
<br>
<br>

**reaction** acceptedOfferClaimsListing\
**when**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Offering.accept(offerer, resource)\
**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Claiming.claim(claimer: offerer, resource: resource)
<br>
<br>

**reaction** startTransaction\
**when**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Claiming.claim(buyer, listing)\
**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Listing: seller is the seller of listing\
**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;TransactionConfirming.start(payer: buyer, payee: seller, subject: Listing)
<br>
<br>

**reaction** completeClaim\
**when**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;TransactionConfirming.confirmReceived(seller, listing)\
**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Claiming: buyer is the claimer of listing\
**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Claiming.complete(claimer: buyer, resource: listing)
<br>
<br>

**reaction** removeAvailableListing\
**when**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Requesting.removeListing(seller, listing)\
**where**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Listing: seller owns listing\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Claiming: listing is available\
**then**\
&nbsp;&nbsp;Listing.remove(listing)
<br>
<br>

# Notes
The four concepts divide the senior sale workflow into separate pieces of behavior. **Listing** represents the items sellers make available and the information buyers use to discover them. **Claiming** controls who currently has exclusive access to an item and prevents multiple buyers from claiming the same item at once. **Offering** handles OBO sales by allowing buyers to make and revise offers and allowing one offer to be accepted. **TransactionConfirming** records the agreement between a buyer and seller that an external payment has been sent and received; the app itself does not process or hold money.

The concepts are connected through reactions rather than depending directly on one another. Creating a Listing makes that listing available in Claiming. For an ordinary sale, a buyer claims the listing directly. For an OBO sale, accepting an offer causes that offerer to claim the listing. A successful claim starts a TransactionConfirming transaction, and the claim is completed once the seller confirms receiving payment.

The generic `Resource` type in both **Claiming** and **Offering**, and the `Subject` type in **TransactionConfirming**, are instantiated with the `Listing` individuals created by **Listing**. `Seller`, `Claimer`, `Offerer`, `Payer`, and `Payee` are all instantiated with the application's user type. This allows the same user to play different roles in different interactions without requiring the concepts themselves to know anything about user accounts.
