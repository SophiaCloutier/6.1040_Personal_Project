# UI Sketches

The following sketches show the primary interfaces for browsing, buying, and selling items through Claimed! Click any sketch to open the full-size image.

## Marketplace

[![Marketplace](UI%20Sketches/marketplace.png)](UI%20Sketches/marketplace.png)

*The marketplace allows buyers to search, filter, save, and browse items currently available from MIT sellers.*

## Item Listing

[![Buy an item](UI%20Sketches/buy%20an%20item.png)](UI%20Sketches/buy%20an%20item.png)

*The item page gives a buyer the item's details, seller and pickup information, and the option to claim the item.*

## Buying Activity

[![My activity buying](UI%20Sketches/my%20activity%20buying.png)](UI%20Sketches/my%20activity%20buying.png)

*The Buying tab lets buyers follow the status of a claimed item from reservation and payment through pickup.*

## Selling an Item

[![Sell an item](UI%20Sketches/sell%20an%20item.png)](UI%20Sketches/sell%20an%20item.png)

*The new-listing page lets a seller add photos and enter the information needed to publish an item to the marketplace.*

## Selling Activity

[![My activity selling](UI%20Sketches/my%20activity%20selling.png)](UI%20Sketches/my%20activity%20selling.png)

*The Selling tab gives sellers one place to view and manage their active, reserved, and completed listings.*

## Empty Buying Activity

[![Nothing claimed yet](UI%20Sketches/nothing%20claimed%20yet.png)](UI%20Sketches/nothing%20claimed%20yet.png)

*When a buyer has not claimed anything yet, the Buying tab directs them back to the marketplace to begin browsing.*

## Empty Selling Activity

[![Nothing listed yet](UI%20Sketches/nothing%20listed%20yet.png)](UI%20Sketches/nothing%20listed%20yet.png)

*When a seller has no listings yet, the Selling tab provides a direct path to listing their first item.*
