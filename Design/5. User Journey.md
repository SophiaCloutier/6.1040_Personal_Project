# User Journey 1: Buying an Item

Percy is an MIT student looking for a reading chair. Instead of checking dormspam and multiple PowerPoints, he opens Claimed! and browses without an account. He searches for a chair and filters by tags, price, and availability. For clothing, he could also filter by size.

He opens Sophia's $24 chair listing, flips through and zooms into the photos, and checks the description, pickup location, and seller. He creates an account or signs in to message Sophia with a question.

Percy can click “Claim for $24” to reserve the chair immediately. Since Sophia allows offers, he instead sends a $20 offer and tracks it under My activity's Offers tab. He can withdraw a pending offer. The chair stays available until Sophia accepts, which reserves it at $20.

Percy checks the saved price and payment instructions, then pays Sophia directly through Venmo. He uploads the required payment screenshot and clicks “I have paid.” Screenshots are optional unless the seller requires one. Either person can cancel the reservation before payment is reported, making the chair available again.

Percy tracks Reserved, Payment sent, and Confirmed on the item page, accessible from My activity's Buying tab. Once Sophia confirms receipt, the chair is marked Sold. They arrange pickup through private messages. Percy can return to the Messages tab to search conversations and read earlier messages.

# User Journey 2: Selling an Item

Sophia is graduating and wants to sell her chair. After signing in, she clicks “Sell an item,” enters a title, $24 price, description, pickup location, and payment instructions. She selects or creates tags, enables “Allow offers (OBO),” and requires a payment screenshot. Clothing size is optional. She can upload up to five photos, adjust their framing and zoom, or remove them before publishing.

The chair appears under My activity's Selling tab. Sophia can edit the listing until payment is reported, although an existing reservation keeps its agreed terms. She replies to Percy's message and reviews his offer from the listing or Offers tab, where she can accept or decline it.

After accepting, Sophia sees the reservation and waits for payment. She reviews Percy's screenshot, checks Venmo herself, and clicks “Confirm payment received.” The chair becomes Sold immediately, leaves marketplace browsing, and remains in their activity histories. They finish arranging pickup in Messages.
