# Claimed!

MIT senior sales are a great way for students to cheaply reuse items within their community, but the current system makes buyers search through countless PowerPoints while sellers juggle emails, payments, claims, offers, and pickup across several different platforms.

Claimed! is a marketplace built specifically for MIT senior sales. It preserves what makes senior sales appealing, the local community, cheap secondhand items, and easy dorm-to-dorm exchanges, while removing much of the friction created by the current dormspam and PowerPoint system.

## Key Features
**Searchable Marketplace**\
Instead of opening individual PowerPoints, buyers can browse all available items in one place and filter them by categories such as clothing, furniture, appliances, size, price, and condition. This makes it much easier for buyers to find items they actually want while allowing sellers' items to reach students who might otherwise never find their sale.

**Claim & Reserve**\
 When a buyer claims an item, Claimed! temporarily reserves it while they pay the seller through Venmo or Zelle. The buyer then marks the item as paid, the seller confirms the payment, and the item becomes claimed. This gives buyers clear feedback about whether an item is actually available and saves sellers from handling duplicate payments and refunds manually.

**Structured OBO**\
 Sellers can mark an item as "or best offer" and collect offers through the marketplace rather than managing them through scattered messages. Buyers can clearly see how and when offers will be decided, while sellers can compare offers in one place, making OBO sales easier and less confusing for everyone.
